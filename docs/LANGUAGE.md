# Language

## Names

- The site is **warbag**, lowercase in running text.
- **App names** come from western / cowboy slang or lineage. They should be short, easy to say and spell, and a little funny. Each app's name sits beside a plain description in the launcher (e.g. "Gyfte · Gift lists") so nobody has to decode it.
- **Interface labels are plain.** The slang stops at the app name. Buttons and messages use ordinary words.

## Access words (same meaning everywhere)

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| Private | Only you. |
| Shared | Specific people or groups you chose. |
| Public | Anyone with the link, no account needed. |
| Invite | Ask someone to join warbag or a group. |
| View | Can see it. |
| Contribute | Can add to it (e.g. add items, claim a gift). |
| Manage | Can change settings and sharing. |

## Gyfte places

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| My list | Lists you look after: yours and your kids'. |
| Family | Your group's people and their lists. |
| I'm getting | Gifts you said you'd get (your claims). |
| Manage | A closed section for organizer tools (groups, import, kids, family link). |

## Mosey plan words

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| Tentative | Probably happening, not settled. |
| Inquiry sent | We asked (a booking request, an email) and are waiting to hear. |
| Confirmed | Settled, nothing to book (a dinner at home, a plan everyone agreed). |
| Booked | Reserved or paid with someone outside the family. |
| Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Food & drink | What kind of meal a stop or idea is. Food & drink is anything else to eat or drink (takeout, a grocery run). |
| Who's handling it | Who's cooking, bringing or booking a meal. |
| Options · pick one | Two or more stops on a day that compete; each person picks one. |
| Pick this / Your pick | Choosing one option for yourself; tap your pick again to clear it. |
| Who picked what | Everyone's pick for a set of options. |
| Ungroup | Turns options back into ordinary stops, keeping picks. |
| People manager | Can invite people and manage groups and app access, but not their own access, admins, removing people or deleting groups. |
| Member (trip) | Sees the trip and its money, adds ideas and expenses, suggests a day. |
| Co-organizer | Does everything the owner can on a trip except share it or delete it. |
| Kid | On the trip's people list, the plan and who's going; left out of money splits unless ticked in (their share goes to their household); never pays. |
| All adults | The split shortcut when a trip has kids (otherwise "Everyone"). |
| Going / Maybe | Who's coming to a stop, from the trip's people list. Not marked is neither. |
| Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Time TBD / Any time | When, without an exact time. |

## Style

- Short by default. Sentence case for labels and headings.
- Actions are verbs: Save, Share, Archive, Delete, Invite.
- No em dashes in interface copy. No emoji in the interface. No jokes in errors.
