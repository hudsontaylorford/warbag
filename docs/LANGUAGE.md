# Language

## Names

- The site is **warbag**, lowercase in running text.
- **App names** come from western / cowboy slang or lineage. They should be short, easy to say and spell, and a little funny. The launcher shows names only; the plain description (e.g. "Gyfte · Gift lists") appears where access is granted (People), so nobody has to decode a name there.
- **Interface labels are plain.** The slang stops at the app name. Buttons and messages use ordinary words.

## Access words (same meaning everywhere)

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| Private | Only you. |
| Shared | Specific people or groups you chose. |
| Public | Anyone with the link, no account needed. |
| Invite | Ask someone to join warbag or a group. |
| View | Can see it. |
| Contribute | Can add to it (e.g. add items, claim a gift). |
| Manage | Can change settings and sharing. |

## Gyfte places

## Mosey plan words

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| Tentative | Probably happening, not settled. |
| Inquiry sent | We asked (a booking request, an email) and are waiting to hear. |
| Confirmed | Settled, nothing to book (a dinner at home, a plan everyone agreed). |
| Booked | Reserved or paid with someone outside the family. |
| Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Food & drink | What kind of meal a stop or idea is. Food & drink is anything else to eat or drink (takeout, a grocery run). |
| Who's handling it | Who's cooking, bringing or booking a meal. |
| Options · pick one | Two or more stops on a day that compete; each person picks one. In the one-line-per-day list a set of options shows once, by its title, flagged "Pick one". |
| Pick this / Your pick | Choosing one option for yourself; tap your pick again to clear it. |
| Who picked what | Everyone's pick for a set of options. |
| Ungroup | Turns options back into ordinary stops, keeping picks. |
| People manager | Can invite people and manage groups and app access, but not their own access, admins, removing people or deleting groups. |
| Admin | Runs all of warbag: people, groups, app access, removing people, making admins, every app. Never opens anyone's lists, notes or trips. Make admin / Remove admin on a person's page. |
| Trip link | A link to a trip anyone can open: the plan and ideas, never money or members-only details; they can ask to join. (Was "View link".) |
| Ask to join / Asking to join | Leaving a name and email on a trip link, or (signed in) asking from a trip's own page; the owner adds or declines. |
| Share link / Copy link | Sending the trip link: Share opens the phone's share sheet; Copy puts it on the clipboard. |
| Join / Joined by link | Joining a trip from its trip link by picking your name, without an account (when the owner allows it). |
| Your link | A person's own private link to a trip they joined by link, to come back on another device. |
| Turn off | Stops something working for good (a trip link, or someone who joined by link). |
| Invite by email | Adding someone to warbag and a trip in one step from Manage. |
| Member (trip) | Sees the trip and its money, adds ideas and expenses, suggests a day. |
| Co-organizer | Does everything the owner can on a trip except share it or delete it. |
| Kid | On the trip's people list, the plan and who's going; left out of money splits unless ticked in (their share goes to their household); never pays. |
| All adults | The split shortcut when a trip has kids (otherwise "Everyone"). |
| Going / Maybe | Who's coming to a stop, from the trip's people list. Not marked is neither. |
| Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Time TBD / Any time | When, without an exact time. |

## Room Tuner words

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| Setup | One speaker in one room: its EQ layout, rounds and presets. |
| Round | One set of recordings (usually 3 spots) made with one EQ on the speaker. Round 1 is with every slider at 0. |
| EQ on the speaker | What the speaker's own app is set to. "I've entered these in the app" and "I put this on the speaker" change it. |
| Preset | Saved slider values, to put on the speaker later. |
| Off target | How far the sound is from the target curve, in dB (lower is better). |
| Good fit | The latest round needs no change worth making: keep what's on the speaker. |
| View link | Anyone with it sees one setup without signing in; can't change anything. |

## Jot words

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| Note | One sticky note. |
| Label | Important, Urgent or Later (or None). "By label" groups notes by it; it never changes your order. |
| Reminder | A time on a note. **Email me** sends one email then; **Only show it on the note** just turns the note red. "Due" once the time has passed. |
| Trash | Where a note goes when you move it there. Kept 30 days; Restore puts it back on top. **Empty trash** deletes everything in it for good. |
| Import from Stickies | Adds notes from the old Stickies app's notes.json or trash.json, as new notes. |

## Getting around

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| ‹ Trips, ‹ People, ‹ Setups, ‹ Money | Up one level in the same app. |
| ‹ Back to … | Back to the page you came from (on People and Account, which you visit on the way). |
| More for this day / Fewer | A day's less common tools (Reorder, Add options, area and tag), folded under "Add to this day". |
| Show all / Show less | Unfolds a long note, and folds it again. |

## Saving

Settings save themselves (no Save button): status reads "Saving…", then "Saved ✓". Save buttons are only for editors that change many fields at once.

## Style

- Short by default. Sentence case for labels and headings.
- Actions are verbs: Save, Share, Archive, Delete, Invite.
- No em dashes in interface copy. No emoji in the interface. No jokes in errors.
